Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Mohan Mishra Multitrade Private Limited (“Mohan Mishra”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Mohan Mishra website store.mohanmishra.app and the Mohan Mishra Android app (package com.app.mmpl) — together, the “Platform”, an online marketplace for buying, renting and enquiring about industrial machinery from verified sellers. We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, mobile number, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), account type (individual/business).
  • Addresses: delivery/site and billing addresses, PIN code, city and state.
  • Business & tax data: GSTIN and business name for GST invoices; for sellers (vendors) also PAN, GSTIN, bank account / UPI details for payouts and KYC documents you upload.
  • Orders & transactions: products ordered, rented or enquired about, quotations, invoices, order status, returns, refunds and wallet/payout records.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We receive the payment status, transaction ID, amount and method type; we do not store full card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking passwords.
  • Communications: support tickets, chat messages, enquiries and reviews you submit.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser type, app version, login history and session records, used for security and fraud prevention. We do not use advertising IDs and do not access your contacts, photos (except files you choose to upload), microphone or precise location.

2. How we use it

  • Create and secure your account, sign-in and OTP verification.
  • Process orders, rentals, quotations, payments, deliveries, installation, returns and refunds.
  • Issue GST-compliant tax invoices and meet tax, accounting and legal obligations.
  • Verify sellers (KYC) and settle seller payouts.
  • Provide customer support and send service messages (order updates, OTPs). Promotional messages are sent only with your consent and you can opt out at any time.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the Platform.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed with:

  • Sellers fulfilling your order (name, delivery address, phone, GSTIN for the invoice).
  • Payment gateway / banks to process payments and refunds.
  • Logistics and installation partners to deliver and install machinery.
  • SMS / email service providers to send OTPs and order notifications.
  • Hosting and cloud providers that store data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government and law-enforcement authorities where required by law (e.g. GST/tax authorities).

4. Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. Order, invoice and payment records are retained for at least 8 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws (GST Act, Companies Act). Security logs are kept for up to 180 days. When retention is no longer required, data is deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to personal data is limited to authorised staff with role-based permissions and audit logs.

6. Your rights

You may access and correct your profile in Account, withdraw consent for marketing, and request deletion of your account and personal data. To delete your account use Account → Request account deletion in the app, the form at /delete-account, or email enterprisesconsolemohanmishra@gmail.com. We will act on requests within 30 days, keeping only records we are legally required to retain.

7. Children

The Platform is meant for businesses and adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change and significant changes will be notified on the Platform.
